summervale — 136 × 76 squares, one TV screen (34 × 19) at a time. Streamed the way the TV would: 512 px cells, only the window and a ring of a quarter screen loaded, a blurry preview under cells still on their way. Arrows move a square, shift + arrow a screen, drag moves in whole squares, M or "Whole map" for the overview (tap it to jump there). Drag the party: crowns open around them, a roof lifts when one steps inside — or tap the roof. Hold the wind dial and drag to turn the wind.
