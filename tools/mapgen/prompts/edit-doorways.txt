The first image is a top-down battle map for a tabletop role-playing game. The second image is
its layout guide: dark grey bands = walls, mid grey = floor, black = rock outside; a gap in a
wall = a doorway.

Edit only the doorways. Keep everything else exactly as it is: same framing, scale and
position of every wall, room, object and texture, same colours and lighting. Do not redraw,
restyle, straighten, crop or zoom the map.

1. In every doorway, remove the thin wooden rail, beam, bar, sill or pair of posts lying
   across the opening, and continue the floor of both rooms straight through it, so the
   doorway is plainly open with nothing in it.
2. Where an opening is wider than its gap in the guide, continue the wall beside it until
   the opening is exactly as wide as the gap; the new piece of wall looks exactly like the
   wall it continues.
